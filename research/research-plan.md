# User Research Plan — Virtual Queue Management App

## Purpose

To understand how students currently experience queueing at campus service points (registry, finance, clinic, accommodation), what frustrates them, and what they'd want from a mobile app that lets them join and track a queue remotely. Findings from this research will directly inform our personas, functional requirements, and any design changes made during the usability-testing/iteration phase.

## Research Questions

1. Which campus service points do students queue at most often, and how long do they typically wait?
2. What is most frustrating about the current (physical) queueing experience?
3. Would students be willing to use a mobile app to join a queue remotely instead of waiting in person? What would make them trust/use it?
4. How do students want to be notified as their turn approaches?
5. What would make a student leave a queue early, and what should happen if they do?

## Method

- **Type:** Written survey/questionnaire (self-administered, distributed digitally — e.g. Google Form or printed handout, exported to this folder as evidence).
- **Participants:** Minimum 5 students, mixing:
  - At least 1 first-year student (represents unfamiliarity with queueing norms)
  - At least 1 final-year/frequent-service-point user (represents efficiency-focused, repeat use)
  - A spread of the four service points (registry, finance, clinic, accommodation) if possible, so no single office dominates the findings
- **Duration:** Survey designed to take under 5 minutes to complete, to maximize response rate and honesty.

## What We'll Do With the Data

- Responses will be logged in `research/03-findings.md`.
- Recurring pain points and quotes will be pulled out and used to justify design decisions (e.g. why live position tracking matters, why notifications matter).
- Two personas (`docs/personas.md`) will be drafted from the patterns observed across the 5+ responses — not invented independently of this data.
